What to evaluate

  1. Toolbar density. Two rows (status, instrument controls) above the plots. Is "Time · Gain · Scale · Trigger" + 4 actions too much? At 560 px it wraps — try the narrow width.
  2. Try SOURCE = quiet −40 dBFS at ×1 (barely visible), then ×4 and Auto. Compare with the flat line today.
  3. Try SOURCE = transient click: Trigger Free → smears. Press 1× Single, then hover the frozen trace for the cursor read-out.
  4. Drag the ▶ trigger marker on the left axis; the waveform re-locks to the new level.
  5. Spectrum: compare Lin vs Log for the 200 Hz-corner case, and Relative vs Absolute (absolute answers "did it get quieter or did the display rescale?").
  6. Meters: SOURCE = clipping latches the CLIP chip and adds a history entry; click a chip or "Reset peaks".
  7. Themes: the dashed/dotted line styles must stay distinguishable in high contrast.

Open questions

  1. Capture length. The emulator sends one block-sized snapshot per message today. ≥ 5 ms/div needs a longer ring buffer (≈ 4096 samples). Where does it live — emulator (protocol change) or webview accumulation?
  2. Trigger in the webview or emulator? Webview trigger is trivial but can miss events between snapshots; only Single-shot needs emulator help.
  3. Keep the spectrum below the scope or make it a tab/toggle to give the scope more height when the panel sits beside code?
  4. Meters column vs inline overlay. Column costs 132 px; could be a thin overlay bar on the scope's right edge.
  5. Export format: CSV only, or WAV too (useful for a null test later)?
  6. Event history overlaps P1-5 (run-state view). Keep it in the scope for now, or wait for the run-state view?
